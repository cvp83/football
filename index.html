Tecmo-style football with all 32 clubs. Four plays: call yours on offense, guess theirs on defense. Kickoffs, punts, field goals and extra points use the power meter.

Controls: drag anywhere on the left side to steer. The big buttons on the right hike, kick, throw (tap a receiver), juke and dive. Best in landscape.
